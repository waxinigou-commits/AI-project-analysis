# Promotion workflow

## Channel roles

| Channel | Best angle | Default format |
|---|---|---|
| 小红书 | Concrete use case and quick payoff | Strong title, short paragraphs, 3–6 relevant tags |
| 知乎 | Decision support and trade-offs | Evidence-led answer or analysis article |
| V2EX | Maker story and technical detail | Direct, transparent, non-salesy community post |
| X | One sharp insight or launch hook | Concise post or short thread |
| LinkedIn | Market insight and professional value | Context, evidence, implication, question |
| 公众号 | Durable tutorial, roundup, or case study | Structured long-form article |
| 即刻 | Timely discovery and personal observation | Conversational short post |

Platform limits and norms change. Verify current requirements before publishing.

## Content packet

For each selected product, create:

1. `core_message`: audience, problem, product promise, proof, limitation.
2. Three headline options with distinct angles.
3. One channel-specific draft per requested platform.
4. One canonical CTA URL with channel-specific UTM parameters.
5. Suggested visual brief, not an invented product screenshot.
6. Disclosure text where there is sponsorship, affiliation, or an incentive.

## UTM convention

Use stable lowercase values:

```text
utm_source=<platform>
utm_medium=organic_social
utm_campaign=ai_product_radar_<yyyy_mm>
utm_content=<product_slug>_<angle>
```

Do not add tracking parameters if the destination owner has asked not to use them.

## Quality checklist

- The product name, URL, pricing, and availability match verified sources.
- The post offers information beyond a bare link or generic praise.
- The hook is not misleading or sensationalized.
- No fabricated numbers, testimonials, customers, rankings, or urgency.
- No impersonation, astroturfing, fake grassroots stories, or undisclosed paid promotion.
- Drafts are meaningfully adapted to each platform.
- Hashtags are relevant and limited; no keyword stuffing.
- Claims about competitors are fair and attributable or labeled as analysis.
- The CTA describes what the reader will get after clicking.
- The draft status is visible.

## Approval table

Before any publish action, show:

| Field | Required value |
|---|---|
| Platform | Exact destination platform |
| Account | Visible account identity |
| Copy | Exact final text |
| Link | Final destination including UTM |
| Media | Exact attached asset or `none` |
| Disclosure | Exact text or `not required` with reason |
| Timing | Publish now or scheduled timestamp |

One approval may cover a clearly enumerated batch. Changes after approval require re-approval if they materially alter claims, destination, account, media, or timing.

## Measurement

Record published URL and, when the user supplies or authorizes access to metrics:

- impressions or views;
- profile visits;
- link clicks;
- saves/bookmarks;
- meaningful comments;
- signups or other destination conversions.

Compare by channel and content angle. Do not equate impressions with qualified demand.
